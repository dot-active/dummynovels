Chapter Ten: A Meeting

That day, in preparation for welcoming Anmoruo and Kangnanaizi, Su He took great care with her appearance, dressing herself with unusual elegance. She even applied a floral ornament between her brows, and amid all the pearls and kingfisher-blue hairpins crowning her head, she made sure not to forget to tuck in that small comb as well. Once satisfied with how she looked, she brought the shopgirl along and climbed into the carriage, heading for Kangnawu's spice shop.

Anmoruo and Kangnanaizi had arrived in Chang'an early that morning. The two of them first returned to their own shop to unload their goods and wash up, ridding themselves of the dust and exhaustion of their long journey, before tidying themselves properly and going to see her parents and Su He.

The moment Su He stepped down from the carriage, she saw Miwei standing radiantly before the shop, waiting for her. Miwei took her by the hand and hurried her inside the spice shop, where Anmoruo and Kangnanaizi came forward at once, warmly greeting her as "Miss Su."

Su He studied the two of them carefully. Anmoruo had dark hair, thick brows, deep-set eyes, and a high, sharply defined nose, along with a neatly trimmed short mustache; he was tall and sturdily built, with a pair of brown, pointed-toe boots on his feet. Kangnanaizi bore a strong resemblance to her mother, with the same deep-set eyes and high, striking nose. Her dark hair was braided into several strands threaded through with real pearls; around her neck hung a necklace alternating turquoise and white jade, and gold earrings dangled from her ears. Su He stared, utterly dumbstruck, feeling as though the whole room had suddenly brightened before her eyes.

Here, at last, was a woman who had truly seen the wide open sky.

Kangnanaizi came forward first, taking Su He's hand and examining her just as closely in turn. Her eye immediately caught the small comb amid all the pearls and hairpins crowning Su He's head, and she pointed at it. "How lovely and unusual."

Su He knew exactly what she meant, and smiled. "A token from A-Cheng."

"A-Cheng?" Kangnanaizi paused for a moment, then understood at once.

"Ah, a sweetheart..." She smiled and pointed to the necklace at her own throat. "This, too, was a gift from my own sweetheart." She glanced back at Anmoruo, and the two of them exchanged a smile.

Not to be left out, the shopgirl chimed in from the side. "Master treats Miss ever so well — he's terrified that if she ever ran off, he'd be left without a wife at all."

Su He rolled her eyes and sighed. "Say another word and I'll have Master dock your wages." At that, the girl fell sheepishly silent.

"It really was a stroke of luck, meeting you — a true weaver from Jinguan City. Not only did it expose that fraud for what he was, it also saved both my money and my reputation. I'm deeply grateful to you and to Fifth Young Master Du. I've just examined those fifteen bolts of Shu brocade — the patterns are exquisite, the sheen simply dazzling. Once Fifth Young Master Du returns to Chang'an again, my wife and I would like to travel back with you to Jinguan City ourselves, to see the weaving workshop properly and discuss the details of our future partnership." At this, Anmoruo rose and bowed to Su He in courteous greeting.

"Of course! Since ancient times, Yizhou has been praised as 'a land blessed by Heaven.' You're both most welcome in Jinguan City, and I'll see to it you're treated as honored guests. And if you find something you like, I'll weave whatever Shu brocade you need from then on."

"But... Kangnanaizi, you've traveled the Silk Road yourself, journeyed far more than a thousand li, and you're Sogdian besides — why did you choose to settle down here in Chang'an?" Su He couldn't hold the question back any longer; it was something that had puzzled her all this while.

"Business aside, home is wherever Father and Mother are!" Kangnanaizi smiled, then added, "Before Mother came to Chang'an, I still had two homes to call my own. Now that both my parents are here, Chang'an is Kangnanaizi's home."

So it was that Su He, who longed with all her heart for distant, faraway worlds, and Kangnanaizi, who had truly seen those distant worlds with her own eyes, wanted nothing more than to stay close by her parents' side.

Watching this family reunited before her, Su He suddenly found herself missing her own father and mother, and the whole crowd of aunties back at the Su family workshop, and even the lotus pond in front of her own home. It was because of that very lotus pond, after all, that her father had given her the name "He" — Lotus — in the first place...
