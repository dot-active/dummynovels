Chapter Five: The Garden Residence

When Master Wu and his wife walked into the courtyard, they hurriedly dropped to the ground and bowed low to the Heir. Zhu Changqing hastily helped the two elders up. The old couple would never have dreamed in their sleep that their family could one day win the favor of the Heir of Huai, who was willing to lodge in their garden residence. It was nothing less than ancestral graves sending up green smoke.

"These past years this old man has been unwell, and I have handed the business and everything else over to Jingyu to manage completely. If the Heir needs anything at all, just tell Jingyu," Master Wu said respectfully and politely.

"No wonder Miss Wu, so young, works with such capability and brisk efficiency, with a good deal of the air of the head of a household."

"This old man has specially engaged a famous chef of Jingdezhen. Whatever the Heir wishes to eat each day, just tell the kitchen. Though it can't match the delicacies of a princely palace, their skill is quite good."

"Master Wu has gone to such trouble. Whatever your household eats, I will eat. There's no need to prepare anything special for me." Zhu Changqing felt a little embarrassed. With all this being arranged, his original intent of not disturbing the people seemed to be "growing more and more distant" from him.

"It's nothing. Whenever a distinguished guest comes to stay at the Wu residence, we always find a chef specially. This has been our custom for many years, so the Heir need not feel constrained. And those cooks like coming to the Wu residence too. Even a thousand-li horse needs a Bo Le to recognize it, so both sides are happy and satisfied." Master Wu smiled as he "persuaded" Zhu Changqing.

"Master Wu's generous hospitality is truly hard to refuse. If I declined any further, I'd seem to be keeping people at arm's length." Zhu Changqing cupped his hands and bowed to Master Wu and his wife, accepting their kindness with courtesy.

Master Wu and his wife invited Zhu Changqing inside to be served tea, while Wu Jingyu gathered all the servants of the garden residence and had them come to meet the Heir. She also picked a steward to wait on call before the main courtyard, so that whatever the Heir needed, he could immediately pass it on to the people concerned. She told Zhi'er to personally make up the Heir's bed, with bedding, pillows and washing supplies all provided, and reminded the steward that hot tea must be kept ready in the teapot on the table at all times, and that there must be no neglect. As Zhu Changqing drank his tea, he watched Wu Jingyu direct the staff calmly and carefully, and felt she was deft and proper, with the manner of a true head of household.

After keeping Zhu Changqing company over tea for a while, Master Wu and his wife took their leave of the Heir. Jingyu came over to drink a few mouthfuls of tea herself and asked whether the Heir felt anything else was lacking. Zhu Changqing was astonished. Jingyu had already arranged everything here "watertight." In so short a time, everything he could think of and everything he couldn't had been prepared neatly and properly. Short of moving the whole princely palace over, there was no residence more sumptuous and comfortable, and she still felt it wasn't enough?

"Do I, the Heir, look hard to please?" Zhu Changqing shook his head with a soft laugh. "Jingyu has seen to things too thoroughly. If you keep going to such trouble, I'm afraid I'll have to go back to the inn."

"The Heir is joking. Who ever lets a guest lack for anything while staying at the Wu residence? That is not how the Wu household treats its guests." Wu Jingyu took another sip of tea. "Heir, don't flee back to the inn. The Wu residence would lose a great deal of face."

Zhu Changqing didn't know whether to laugh or cry. Now his wish not to disturb the people had "hit a wall," and this girl of only sixteen or seventeen before him had sealed off his "retreat" so tightly that there was no longer any possibility of "turning back." So he had no choice but to rise and bow to Wu Jingyu. "It seems I can only impose on the whole Wu household."

"Please rest easy, Heir! This is the household's supreme honor!" Wu Jingyu returned the bow and added, "When lunch is brought up, Jingyu will look over the dishes to see if they suit you before she leaves."

"Why don't you stay and dine with me, Jingyu? This afternoon I'd like to see the porcelain workshops, and we could go together."

"Very well." Wu Jingyu had hesitated at first, since dining at the same table as the Heir was not in keeping with propriety. But the Heir had spoken, and personally tasting the chef's work to see whether it suited the Heir's palate was also a matter of importance to attend to, so she decided that obeying was more respectful than declining.
