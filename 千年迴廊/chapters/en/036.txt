Chapter Eleven: Homeward Bound

Du Cheng had already been in Chang'an for seven days on this second trip. Su He had packed her belongings early, and had the household staff carry them out to the carriages one by one. Anmoruo and Kangnanaizi, for their part, traveled light this time, nothing like the grand procession of their journeys along the Silk Road — just a few guards and two or three carriages following behind, ready to set out together. Kangnawu and Miwei had come specially to see them off, and for a while the front of the Du family shop was full of lively bustle.

Miwei sent Su He off with some agarwood and benzoin, along with several dozen hu bing flatbreads to keep her and the others fed and satisfied on the road. She had also had a set of clothes tailored specially to Su He's measurements, styled after the vivid, colorful garments Kangnanaizi had worn the first time they met. Su He found the patterns and material quite different from Shu brocade, yet strikingly beautiful all the same, and thought she might study it carefully — perhaps she could weave a Shu brocade pattern that would appeal even more to Hu tastes.

Accepting Miwei's heartfelt gift, Su He waved goodbye to Kangnawu and Miwei with great reluctance in her heart.

"I'm heading home now. I'll miss you both. If I ever have the chance to come back to Chang'an, I'll be sure to visit — please take good care of yourselves, both of you."

"We will, and you take care too. When you marry, don't forget to send word — your uncle and I will absolutely come to Jinguan City for the wedding feast." Tears welled in Miwei's eyes; this child Su He truly was wonderful, and had been her greatest companionship these past several months.

At the mention of marriage, Du Cheng perked up at once, his face lighting with unmistakable delight. "Absolutely, without question. You should see my fifteen-year-old younger brother, Sixth Young Master — he's already started running with the caravan too! Father means for him to take over some of my work, so that once I'm married, I'll be able to spare more time to spend with A-He, don't you think?" He gestured toward Du Yun, who was busy loading goods nearby.

Su He was momentarily stunned to hear this, while Kangnawu and Miwei both burst out laughing. Kangnawu added at once, "Sounds like good news is right around the corner for these two."

"Father says that once we're back home this time, we'll go ahead and arrange the engagement." Du Cheng, too pleased with himself to notice, was caught completely off guard by the sharp glare Su He shot his way.

"How can you be so utterly shameless, saying something like that without the slightest embarrassment?"

"What's there to be embarrassed about? If I don't hurry and get you settled, who's to say some Twenty-First or Twenty-Second Young Master won't come along one of these days — then wouldn't I be the one worrying myself grey?"

"Stop talking already..." Su He struck him lightly, displeased, her face flushing with embarrassment.

"We should get going soon — if we don't leave now, we might as well just stay here." Du Yun, having checked over everything that needed loading onto the carriages, came running up to them.

"Well then... let's go." Su He waved once more to Kangnawu and Miwei, then turned and waved to Anmoruo and Kangnanaizi, signaling that it was time to set out.

Kangnanaizi and Anmoruo also came forward to take their leave of her parents, and Kangnanaizi embraced Miwei with visible reluctance to part before finally climbing into the carriage alongside Anmoruo.

The morning light was lovely, and the parting bittersweet. Having seen Chang'an, Su He had gathered a rich harvest of experience, carrying it all, full and overflowing, close to her heart; and having met Kangnanaizi, she no longer envied distant horizons alone — the future, she now understood, lay in her own hands.
