Chapter 12: Peace Torn Apart, an Incursion of Raw Power

    The tranquility of the living room was severed without warning by the sound of the doorbell.

    That shrill electronic chime felt jarringly out of place in the spacious living room, like a heavy hammer smashing apart the warm, homely atmosphere of just a moment before. An Yilin's arms tightened slightly around An Yuning, her expression still somewhat stunned. An Yuchen, who had already been half-leaning against Ji Yunshen's shoulder, drifting toward sleep, blinked his eyes open groggily at the sound and glanced toward the door.

    Shen Tinglan was the first to stand, her brow furrowing almost imperceptibly. "Who could it be, at this hour?"

    An Jingchuan headed toward the entryway, his tone missing its usual note of playfulness. "I'll go check."

    Low voices could soon be heard from outside the door, two tones faintly tinged with anger. Before long, An Jingchuan came back, his face grim, his voice pressed low. "It's Li Yu'en's parents. Someone told them you had the children. They're here now."

    The instant those words fell, the living room seemed to go still all at once.

    An Yuchen flinched slightly, and An Yuning instinctively burrowed deeper into An Yilin's arms. Just then, the moment An Jingchuan pulled the door open even a crack, Li Yu'en's parents came barging in, radiating fury.

    Lin Shuhua's gaze landed almost instantly on the child in An Yilin's arms, her expression twisted, and she advanced with overwhelming aggression: "Where's the child? Hand the child over!"

    An Yilin, holding the child, took a step back, her face pale, yet she didn't yield an inch.

    An Jingche and An Jingheng, moving with precise, synchronized timing, stepped forward together in the same instant, flanking An Yilin left and right like twin towers. An Jingche's eyes were icy cold, as if ready at any moment to neutralize any threat: "Mrs. Li, keep your distance."

    Ji Yunshen, meanwhile, calmly moved behind An Yilin, his eyes deep and dark as a frozen pool, every muscle in his body drawn tight, his gaze locked unwaveringly on Lin Shuhua, ready to bring her under total control the instant she made a single move.

    Li Zhengde stood to the side, his face dark. "Since the blood running through that child's veins belongs to the Li family, there's no reason for them to stay outside it. Today, we're taking them home with us, no matter what."

    Lin Shuhua stared at the child, her eyes reddening with heat, which quickly twisted into an almost fanatical resentment. "An Yilin, I've already lost one son. Yu'en was my only support. Are you really going to cut off even these two grandchildren, severing every last hope my family has left? How dare you keep the children by your side? If it weren't for you, why would my son be dead? Do you even deserve to be called a mother?"

    Faced with this venomous accusation, An Jingchuan couldn't help but sneer coldly. "You want the children? Where were you when Yilin needed you most?"

    Lin Shuhua wasn't listening to any of it. Looking at An Yilin's composed demeanor only sent her resentment surging straight to her head. She let out a shrill screech and, heedless of everything, lunged past An Jingche's line of defense, her fingers curled like a hawk's talons, clawing viciously at An Yilin's face!

    "Give the child back to me—!"

    The sudden attack startled everyone. An Yilin instinctively turned her body aside, those long nails barely grazing her neck, leaving behind a thin red scratch.

    But before Lin Shuhua's hand could reach the child, an icy chill had already enveloped her completely.

    Ji Yunshen struck faster than lightning. Rather than shoving her back, he clamped down on Lin Shuhua's wrist with the precision of an iron clamp, freezing her furious assault dead in midair.

    Lin Shuhua couldn't move at all, her face twisting from the searing pain shooting through her wrist.

    Seeing this, An Jingche stepped forward, his low, menacing voice exploding right beside Li Yu'en's parents' ears:

    "This past year, my sister raised them alone, giving them complete love and a sense of safety. And the two of you, the moment you heard about this, the first thing that came to mind wasn't the children's wellbeing — it was 'the Li family bloodline.'"

    An Jingche turned, his gaze sweeping coldly over Li Yu'en's parents, the pressure he carried — steeped for years in the edges of both power and violence — enough to nearly suffocate anyone standing before him:

    "When our two families severed ties back then, it was you who said we'd owe each other nothing. Now, these children belong to Yilin. No one is going to change that."

    An Jingheng added coolly, his tone an unquestionable, professional declaration: "If these two children suffer any physical or psychological trauma because of your harassment today, as their physician, I will personally make sure you understand exactly what legal liability means."

    With her wrist pinned by Ji Yunshen, and facing the mountain-like pressure of the An brothers, Lin Shuhua finally felt despair setting in.

    "You people... you and your raw power..." Li Zhengde's face had turned ashen.

    "This isn't raw power." Ji Yunshen's voice was flat, yet carried an absolute, unquestionable finality. He slowly released his grip, pushing Lin Shuhua back a step in the same motion, shielding An Yilin without a single gap. "This is protection."

    An Jingchuan let out a cold laugh and pressed the call button without hesitation. "Throw them out. From now on, if this couple ever shows up at the front door of the An estate again, no need to announce them — just throw them out directly."

    The bodyguards swept in with brisk efficiency, giving Li Yu'en's parents no chance to struggle at all. As Lin Shuhua was dragged away, she was still screaming curses at An Yilin, but her voice was soon cut off from the night by the closing door.

    The door slammed shut with a heavy thud, yet the oppressive weight inside the house lingered long after.

    An Yilin stood rooted in place, her arms, still holding the child, trembling faintly. An Jingchuan turned to look at her, his expression finally softening somewhat, and said quietly, "It's over now. I'm here."

    Ji Yunshen walked quietly over to her side, his gaze sweeping over the red mark on her neck, a flicker of violence crossing his eyes — but when he turned to face An Yilin, only gentleness remained. "Did that scare you?"

    An Yilin looked at the three brothers before her, and this man, all holding up the sky for her sake, and the fear lodged in her chest was, at last, filled with a sense of security she had never known before.

    She knew that this war — they would keep it out at the door for her.
