Chapter 11: The Trivial, Everyday Business of Returning to the World

    By the time the car pulled up outside the An family estate, the night had grown deep. Only the garden lamps along the courtyard lit up one after another, casting the stone steps and shrubbery in a gentle, quiet glow.

    Gu Yanxi brought the car to a smooth stop and turned to glance at the back seat. An Yilin still held tightly to the folder that symbolized her return to the battlefield, not yet fully pulled out of the state of intense focus that work had put her in.

    "We're here," Gu Yanxi said softly.

    An Yilin came back to herself and nodded gently. "Thanks for the hard work, Yanxi."

    Ji Yunshen's car was parked not far behind. Watching them arrive safely, he didn't step forward right away, waiting instead until Gu Yanxi had walked An Yilin up the stone steps and confirmed she was safely inside before he got out of his own car.

    The two women walked into the house one after the other, and the moment the door opened, a small burst of laughter and chatter drifted out from the living room.

    An Yuchen and An Yuning were sitting on the carpet playing with building blocks. The instant they saw An Yilin walk in, both children looked up at once, letting out delighted cries.

    "Mama!"

    An Yuning wobbled to her feet and threw herself into her mother's arms. An Yuchen followed close behind with his own small, hurried steps. An Yilin's heart softened instantly, and she crouched down to gather them both into her embrace.

    Just as she was asking the children whether they had been good, a low, steady voice sounded from behind her.

    "They were very good today. No fussing, and they ate everything without complaint."

    Ji Yunshen had changed out of his shoes, his gaze settling on her and the children, his expression softer than usual. When An Yuning spotted him, a flicker of delight crossed her eyes, and she reached out her little hands, babbling, "Up! Up!"

    An Jingche, standing on the other side of the living room, glanced once at Ji Yunshen and remarked flatly, "She's quite fond of you."

    Ji Yunshen crouched down and lifted An Yuning into his arms. His movements were very gentle, one hand steady against her back. An Yuning stared up at him with her round eyes, then suddenly smiled and reached out a small hand to touch his face — the touch light, carrying the most direct, unguarded trust of a child.

    On the other side, An Yuchen walked over to An Yilin, and only after confirming his mother was safe did he turn his gaze toward Ji Yunshen. The little boy seemed to be weighing something in his mind, and in the end, he too walked forward step by step, reaching out a small hand to gently grasp the corner of Ji Yunshen's suit jacket.

    Ji Yunshen lowered his eyes to look at him.

    An Yuchen tilted his head back, his expression carrying a child's earnestness. After a few seconds, he finally said, in a small voice, "Uncle... came."

    The words came out a little clumsily, yet they made Ji Yunshen's entire body go still for a moment.

    He crouched down lower, so he could meet the child's eyes at the same level, his voice low and gentle. "Mm. Uncle's here."

    An Yilin stood off to the side, watching this scene, her gaze flickering softly. She knew children didn't grow close to someone without reason. It proved that this man had not only stayed by their side all this time, but had, in every moment she wasn't there, comforted this family in his own quiet way.

    The living room lights were soft and warm. Ji Yunshen held An Yuning in his arms, An Yuchen still gripping the corner of his jacket, and when he lifted his head, he happened to catch An Yilin's gaze looking his way.

    Across the living room, their eyes met briefly.

    Neither of them said a word, but something had already, quietly and without fanfare, settled back into its place.

    Ji Yunshen walked over and set a plate of peeled apple slices down on the table.

    "Be careful not to forget to eat again," he said, his tone light.

    An Yilin looked up at him. Her gaze was no longer so distant. She picked up a slice of apple and quietly took a bite. It was the first time in a very long while that she had accepted his care in front of him the way a truly "living person" would.

    Ji Yunshen understood perfectly well in his heart — she wasn't ready yet to face love; she had only just decided to take up the role of mother. But for him, that was already enough.

    She was moving forward.

    And all he needed to do was follow her pace, standing guard outside the door or within it, never pushing, never forcing.
