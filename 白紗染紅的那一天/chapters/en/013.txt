Chapter 13: Undercurrents Before the Storm

    Inside Ji Yunshen's office, the air pressure was low enough to feel suffocating. Beyond the floor-to-ceiling windows, the city lights blazed, casting their glow across the heavy shadows within the room.

    By the long table, the three An brothers wore grave expressions. An Jingchuan, stripped of his usual teasing manner, sat with a dark face, flipping through the intelligence reports at hand; An Jingche and An Jingheng sat across from him, their eyes sharp as blades, while the assistant in the room kept even their breathing deliberately quiet.

    "The best move right now is to send the children abroad first." An Jingheng was the first to break the silence, his tone carrying a calm yet heavy resolve. "Since the Li family has already come knocking once, they won't give up easily. Staying here any longer is too great a risk."

    "I agree." An Jingchuan tapped the table, his tone impatient. "Better to lie low for a while than let them keep watching Yilin and the children constantly."

    The assistant added immediately, "I've already arranged several absolutely secure safe houses. We can guarantee this will be foolproof."

    Everyone's reasoning was rooted entirely in "safety," which was understandable enough. And yet, at the head of the table, Ji Yunshen's fingers tapped the table slowly and evenly, his expression never once loosening. He raised his eyes and swept them over everyone present — that gaze light as water, yet weighing down like a thousand pounds, freezing the air in the room instantly.

    "No." He spoke, his voice low, yet carrying an unquestionable finality.

    An Jingchuan blinked, straightening up with some agitation. "Yunshen, do you have any idea how dangerous the situation is right now?"

    "I know." Ji Yunshen cut him off, his gaze sharp. "But Yilin has only just managed to pick back up a bit of rhythm in her life. The children are the only thing holding her up right now. If we force the children away at a time like this, what do you think that would do to her?"

    "So we just stay here and wait for the Li family to come again?" An Jingche's brow furrowed slightly.

    "Of course I understand the risk." Ji Yunshen interlaced his fingers, leaning forward slightly, a churning undercurrent pressed down behind his eyes. "But true protection isn't forcing her into a corner where the only way to buy safety is through loss. If we make her feel that same powerlessness, that same sense of being stripped of everything all over again, she will fall apart far faster than any of you can imagine."

    Those words landed like ice water, and the three brothers, previously worked up with urgency, fell silent all at once. In their world of power struggles, they had grown used to sacrificing the pawn to protect the king — but they had forgotten the wound in An Yilin's heart that had never truly healed.

    What Ji Yunshen's words protected was the last shred of dignity An Yilin held onto as a mother.

    Yet the tension in the air didn't last long before An Jingheng sharply caught onto a critical point. He suddenly lifted his head, his gaze cutting toward Ji Yunshen like a blade.

    "Something's wrong."

    "What's wrong?" An Jingchuan frowned.

    An Jingheng slammed the report in his hand down onto the table, his expression turning chillingly cold. "How could the Li family have known Yilin had the children? We never made it public. The hospital kept the information completely sealed. There's no way the Li family could have gotten that information out of thin air."

    The temperature in the office seemed to drop sharply.

    An Jingche's expression darkened instantly. "You're saying... someone deliberately informed them?"

    "What else would you call it, if not deliberate?" An Jingheng's voice carried a note of cold mockery. "The Li family picked their timing with too much precision — they even knew all about Yilin's poor health, even about the pregnancy. This isn't just about the children — this is a 'precision strike' on the An family's security."

    Ji Yunshen had remained silent the entire time, but beneath that deathly calm was a fury burning wildly out of control.

    He slowly rose to his feet, his gaze sweeping across everyone present like a blade.

    "Whether this leaked from the hospital, or from someone close to us," Ji Yunshen said, his voice low, as though ground out from deep in his throat, "within three days, I want to know who this 'messenger' is."

    A flicker of violence crossed An Jingchuan's eyes. "Leave this to me. As long as we keep digging, the fox's tail will show sooner or later."

    An Jingche stared at the table, deep in thought, piecing together all the clues. This scheme had been laid out far bigger than any of them had imagined — whoever it was understood not just the An family's boundaries, but precisely where An Yilin's weakest point lay.

    "Whoever it is, this cannot happen a second time," An Jingheng said coldly.

    Ji Yunshen said nothing further, turning instead to gaze out at the dazzling yet illusory night skyline, his knuckles gripped white. He understood, better than anyone, that the Li family had dared to show up today because they believed they had nothing to fear. If they didn't root out whoever was leaking information from the shadows, the next time, the Li family wouldn't simply be trying to "take the children" — they would press a blade directly against Yilin and the children's throats.

    He would never let anyone destroy this peace he had fought so hard to protect.

    Never.
