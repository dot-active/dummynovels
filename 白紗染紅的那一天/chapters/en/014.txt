Chapter 14: Madness at the Door, the Thunder of the Law

    Inside the An family study, the atmosphere was oppressive enough to feel suffocating. An Jingche and An Jingchuan stood side by side at the desk, the screen flickering with a complex stream of system data.

    "Found it." An Jingchuan's fingers flew rapidly across the keyboard, his brow locked in a deep frown. "There's a clear trail of unauthorized access in the core database, and the target is unmistakable — the children's birth and medical data. Even Yilin's postpartum physical reports were fully accessed."

    An Jingche studied the data, his eyes turning frighteningly cold. "Can we trace who did it? Whoever this is knows the An family's encryption architecture far too well — this is either an inside job, or—"

    Before he could finish piecing the clues together, a flurry of urgent footsteps suddenly sounded outside the door. The butler rushed in, pushing the door open in a panic, his face deathly pale. "Eldest Young Master, Third Young Master! It's bad! Miss is at the front gate, and Mrs. Li has grabbed hold of her! They followed the car back and are causing a scene at the door right now!"

    An Jingche and An Jingchuan exchanged a glance, a chilling wave of violence surfacing in both their eyes at once, and without hesitation, they turned and sprinted toward the front door.

    At the front gate of the estate, the night was torn apart by glaring headlights.

    Ji Yunshen's black sedan had only just pulled into the driveway when the scene before him made his pupils constrict sharply. Lin Shuhua, like a woman gone mad, had her hands locked in a death grip on An Yilin's long hair, her nails nearly digging into her scalp, dragging her whole body toward the Li family's car. An Yilin's face was pale from the pain, but she clung desperately to the child in her arms, protecting them with everything she had.

    "Give the child back to me! This is Li family blood!" Lin Shuhua screeched furiously, her other hand flailing wildly, striking at An Yilin's body.

    "Screech—!"

    Ji Yunshen shoved the car door open and, in an instant, became a blur of ruthless motion. Without the slightest hesitation, he seized Lin Shuhua's wrist directly and, with one precise leverage move, forced her fingers open by sheer force. The bodyguards swarmed in right behind him, forcibly separating Li Yu'en's parents from An Yilin and pinning them hard against the wall.

    Even restrained, Lin Shuhua's fury hadn't cooled in the slightest, and she screamed madly at An Yilin: "You home-wrecking witch! You got my son killed, and now you want to hog the children too? You'll die a horrible death!"

    Just then, An Jingche and An Jingchuan came striding out the door. The moment they saw the few strands of torn hair still clinging to An Yilin's neck and that glaring red mark, An Jingchuan's face turned utterly grim, and he said coldly to the bodyguards, "Deal with these two lunatics. Make it clean."

    "Big brother, third brother..." An Yilin spoke weakly. She lifted her head, tears welling in her eyes, and shook her head at her two brothers. "Let them go. Yuning and Yuchen are still here — I don't want the children to see anything violent..."

    An Jingche looked at his sister's pale yet resolute face, a flicker of tenderness crossing his eyes. He turned and instructed the butler, "Take the children inside first."

    The butler didn't dare delay, hurrying forward to carefully carry An Yuning and An Yuchen back into the house. Only once he'd confirmed the children were safely away from the chaos did An Jingche give a slight nod, casting a glance behind him.

    Su Wanning, the lawyer who had already been waiting at the gate, stepped forward with steady, composed strides.

    In her hand she held a document freshly printed out, her eyes cold and fixed on Li Yu'en's parents, her voice crisp and professional:

    "Mr. Li Zhengde, Ms. Lin Shuhua. I'm Su Wanning, the An family's retained attorney. I have an obligation to inform you both of the relevant legal risks."

    She opened the document, her fingertip landing on the legal clauses. "First, Mr. Li Yu'en and Miss An Yilin never completed a legal marriage registration while he was alive. Under the law, these two children are classified as children born out of wedlock, and the mother, Miss An Yilin, holds full parental rights and custody. Unless the Li family can produce definitive proof of the biological father's expressed wishes before his death, you hold no legal visitation rights or custodial obligations toward these two children whatsoever."

    "Second, the physical assault you just committed against Miss An Yilin already constitutes bodily harm under the Criminal Code. Combined with your threatening behavior in public and your forced entry into a private residence today, if we report this to the police right now, both of you will face criminal charges immediately."

    Su Wanning closed the document, her gaze carrying a deep, quiet menace as she looked directly at the couple before her:

    "The two of you have already lost one son. I imagine you wouldn't want to spend the rest of your lives behind bars — nor would you want your remaining daughter to lose both her parents because of this."

    Lin Shuhua was stunned into silence by that ice-cold threat, her face turning white as paper. Seeing this, Li Zhengde, well aware he stood no chance against the An family's overwhelming legal and social power, quickly pulled Lin Shuhua, who was still trying to hurl more curses, and turned to leave in a wretched hurry.

    The night settled back into quiet. An Yilin stood where she was, taking a deep breath. She knew that only by building this kind of defense could she protect these two children, so hard-won, now finally back at her side.
