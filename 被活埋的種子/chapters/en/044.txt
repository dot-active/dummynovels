The Devoted Gardener, Part Two
The crime Chen Daipeng had hidden for fifteen years was finally exposed in front of the whole school.
And Fang Chuchu's tragedy did not end there:
"After I was 'disappeared,' Chen Daipeng went to my homeroom teacher at the time, Wang Qin, to clear himself of suspicion. Wang Qin had a girl who walked home with me every day give false testimony, saying I had gone straight home after school and hadn't stayed behind. The girl who lied back then was your biology teacher, Huang Lu. Because she helped Wang Qin with that lie, Wang Qin paid her tuition all the way to graduation and then got her a job at this school. And Wang Qin, for her part, won the principal's favor, and every year she was given the class with the best grades. Everyone ended up happy.
"In the end, their evil was blamed on a natural disaster. Everyone told the same story: I had disappeared in the earthquake. Speaking of that earthquake — people were killed and injured in it, but it saved me. As I lay in the coffin waiting in despair to die, the earthquake cracked the sewer beneath it. The coffin fell into the sewer and broke apart, and I walked out of it alive.
"To survive, I drank the filthy water underground and ate sewer rats. At last, one day, I dug through what was blocking my way and found a way out. But when I, with no one to help me, tried to go back to my mother, she had already been forcibly committed to a psychiatric hospital.
"Chen Daipeng could not only shut me in a coffin; he could shut my mother away in a psychiatric hospital. I despaired again. I had escaped the coffin and escaped the school, but I was still in danger. If I showed myself, I would face not only Chen Daipeng and this school, but all of Chen Daipeng's connections and social resources.
"So I got on a train, fled Tiecheng, changed my name, and started over in another city. But I never forgot the injustice I had suffered in Tiecheng. I had plastic surgery and changed my name to Hui Nan — hui, 'return'; nan, 'girl' — meaning that the girl nailed into the coffin would one day come back.
"I wasn't at this school, but I watched it in secret all along, waiting for my chance. Then one day I heard the news: a student named Gu Qing had hanged herself in a classroom. It was the perfect opportunity to come back in a dead girl's shape. I immediately applied for a job here and spent half a year working out my revenge.
"Ma Dahua kept me at school at night, so I stole her keys and kept her at school too. Xiao Jin drugged my food, so I drugged hers. Du Qiping nailed me into a coffin, so I nailed him into one. Huang Lu hadn't seen me go home but said she had, so I put out her eye. Oh, and 'First pockmark hangs herself, second pockmark watches' — Huang Lu taught me that rhyme back when we were at school. It made a perfect script for sending them on their way. Wickedness has its price, and every one of them has paid the debt they owed. So, teachers out there, I hope that while you demand respect from your students, you'll respect your students too. However you treat your students now is how they'll treat you later. Isn't that right? The world is fair after all."
As she said these last words, Fang Chuchu's gaze turned to Chang Di in the crowd below. She had indeed proved to this student the "fairness" she had once spoken of. Sirens suddenly sounded outside the school; the police had received the call and arrived, and several officers were already rushing through the gate. Hui Nan seemed not to notice, and went on speaking calmly into the microphone:
"Principal Chen, the devoted gardener. Everyone saw you planting flowers in that flowerbed every day. Only today have they learned why you planted flowers there, and what you really planted."
Fang Chuchu paused. Her eyes suddenly reddened. At last, through gritted teeth, she said, one word at a time: "I am the seed you buried in the earth fifteen years ago. And now it is time for you to reap the harvest."
The dagger in Fang Chuchu's hand suddenly thrust toward Chen Daipeng's throat. Two shots rang out across the campus, and the dagger stopped in midair. Chen Daipeng, on his knees, broke out in a cold sweat. The one who died was not him. It was Fang Chuchu, collapsed beside him. The student he had failed to kill fifteen years ago died today, in the same place, under police fire.
At the moment Fang Chuchu fell, the phone in her pocket lit up. A text message had arrived: "Dear parent of Fang Wang, this is Fang Wang's homeroom teacher. Fang Wang has not been to school for a week, and I would like to ask you about the situation."
