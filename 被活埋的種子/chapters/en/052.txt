Tang Sui's Prototype — Tang Hui, the "Petitioning Mother"

Tang Hui, the "petitioning mother," is the mother of the victim in the "Yongzhou case of the eleven-year-old girl forced into prostitution." In 2006, Lele, only eleven years old, was raped and gang-raped by several men, forced into prostitution, and abused throughout. Tang Hui did everything she could to rescue her daughter and demanded that a case be opened and investigated. Yet despite the crime's appalling nature, the police let it drop. Tang Hui petitioned repeatedly, and finally, in 2008, the case was prosecuted in the local court; the offenders received sentences including death and life imprisonment.
On August 3, 2012, the Lingling District Public Security Bureau in Yongzhou, Hunan Province, sentenced Tang Hui to "eighteen months of re-education through labor" for "disturbing social order." When this came to light it caused a huge public outcry, and under pressure from all sides, Tang Hui was released. Refusing to accept the labor re-education decision, Tang Hui sued the Yongzhou Re-education Through Labor Committee. On July 15, 2013, she finally won on appeal and was awarded a little over two thousand yuan in compensation — but received no written apology.
