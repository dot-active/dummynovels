The Girl in the School Uniform
In the graveyard on the back slope of White Stone Mountain, Zhu Hua begged without stopping from inside the pit, pleading with Wang Qin to spare her. Wang Qin seemed to hear nothing. Mechanically repeating the same simple motion, she shoveled earth into the pit, one spadeful after another.
"I'm the one who put the acid in your cup." When the earth had covered Zhu Hua's shoulders, she finally confessed. And at last Wang Qin stopped her shovel, looked down at her coldly, and said, "Why?"
"I'm sorry, Teacher Wang. Greed blinded me; I did a terrible thing. At this school, the only two of us who've always taught the fourth-year classes are you and me, and every time, the leadership gave you the class with the best grades. Our students only get a handful of places at the key high schools. Every year, not one student from my class got into a key high school. I'm nearly at retirement age and I still haven't been made a senior teacher. All I wanted was to get senior-teacher rank so my pension would be a bit bigger. I never meant to kill you, honestly. I only wanted to use the serial killings as cover to hurt you a little, so you'd retire early and I could get the good class. I only wanted senior-teacher rank! I never meant to kill anyone! Let me go!"
By the end, Zhu Hua was begging loudly again.
When she had heard Zhu Hua out, Wang Qin picked up the shovel again and said coldly, "It's too late. You've seen me. I can't let you live. I can't let anyone know I'm not dead." As she spoke, another shovelful of earth landed on Zhu Hua's head.
In that instant Zhu Hua seemed to understand something. She cried out, "It's you! You're the murderer! You killed Tang—" The earth finally covered her face, and she said no more.
The pit was filled in, level with the ground around it. If you walked over it, you would never guess there was a living person buried beneath.
Wang Qin put down the shovel and walked to the edge of the cliff. The view was good here; you could see the distant mountains. She sighed and said coldly to the empty air, "That's right — I killed Tang Sui. Then I sent a text in her voice to mislead the police, burned her face with acid, dressed her in my clothes so she'd pass for my corpse, and stole her body before the forensic examiner came in to take DNA. That made it certain I had been killed. I fooled everyone. I even fooled Death."
"Why did you kill her?"
"I killed her because…"
Wang Qin froze mid-sentence. Zhu Hua was buried in the earth. So who had asked?
Wang Qin spun around. Standing where Zhu Hua had just been buried was a girl — a teenage girl in a school uniform.
At the sight of her, Wang Qin felt a fear such as she had never known, because she understood that she had not fooled Death after all. A slight, pretty young girl had brought her terror to its peak — because this girl was the student who had disappeared fifteen years ago: Tang Sui's daughter, Fang Chuchu.
"Chu… Chuchu." When Wang Qin spoke, her voice was already shaking. "These… these fifteen years, wh-where have you been?"
The moment the words were out, Wang Qin realized something horrifying: this student who had been a teenager fifteen years ago was still a teenager now! There was no way her face could have stayed exactly the same for fifteen years. Unless she wasn't human!
"She never killed anyone. Why did you kill her?"
The "Fang Chuchu" asked again, staring straight into Wang Qin's eyes and walking toward her step by step.
"Why did you kill her?"
"Why did you kill her?"
"Why did you kill her?"
The "Fang Chuchu"'s voice grew louder and louder, her steps quicker and quicker, and she drew closer and closer to Wang Qin.
"No, don't, Fang Chuchu, don't come any closer… No!… Ah!"
Wang Qin backed away step by step. Terror made her forget the sheer drop behind her. Her foot found nothing, and she fell from the cliff with a bloodcurdling scream.
The "Fang Chuchu" looked at the place where Wang Qin had gone over the edge and said softly, "You still haven't told me why you killed her."
