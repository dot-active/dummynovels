Evidence, Part Two
In the hospital ward, Chen Daipeng, who had sunk into despair, was suddenly filled with confidence in life again. Fang Chuchu had killed all the witnesses. With no witnesses, naturally there could be no conviction. After Jin the lawyer left, Chen Daipeng gloated to himself and began to dream of the future. With his connections, it would be easy to get the media to play the whole thing down. Once he was out of hospital, even if he couldn't be a principal anymore, he could land some post or other in the Education Bureau and live out the rest of his life in luxury.
Footsteps outside the ward broke into his thoughts. The door opened and two police officers came in: Chief Wu Xian and his assistant.
"Principal Chen, there are a few things we need you to explain. We hope you'll cooperate." Wu Xian stood at the foot of the bed, looking at Chen Daipeng out of the corner of his eye.
Chen Daipeng found Wu Xian's manner very unpleasant. The last time this officer had spoken to him, he had been all deference; in so short a time he had put on an entirely different face, like a cat eyeing the mouse under its paw.
But his conversation with Jin the lawyer had given Chen Daipeng backbone. "Chief Wu, I have nothing to explain. I've never done anything illegal or improper. If you're asking about the night Fang Chuchu disappeared fifteen years ago, I'm sorry, it was too long ago; I can't recall any details. If you have any evidence from that case fifteen years ago, I'll gladly cooperate. If not, please don't disturb my rest. I'm a victim too."
Wu Xian gave a cold laugh. "Principal Chen, I've come to see you today mainly about another case."
Chen Daipeng: "Another case?"
Wu Xian: "We found a charred body on the back slope of White Stone Mountain. Your fingerprints were on the wooden sword stuck in the body's neck and on the bottle of scented oil beside it. Please explain."
For a moment Chen Daipeng couldn't take it in, and blurted out, "The one I burned — wasn't it a ghost?"
Wu Xian: "A ghost?"
Chen Daipeng: "A ghost, yes! I saw her with my own eyes, digging, trying to burrow back into the ground!"
Wu Xian: "I'm sorry, Principal Chen. She was a person. She was digging to save Zhu Hua, whom Wang Qin had buried in the earth."
Chen Daipeng felt as though he had been struck by lightning. The beautiful dream of a moment ago burst like a bubble. Of course — Fang Chuchu hadn't died fifteen years ago, so the little girl in the school uniform couldn't possibly have been Fang Chuchu's ghost. She was just an innocent girl, burned to death.
"B-but… she… why did she look so much like Fang Chuchu fifteen years ago?" The sudden shock had left Chen Daipeng incoherent.
Wu Xian: "The reason is simple. That girl was the evidence that you raped Fang Chuchu fifteen years ago."
Chen Daipeng froze for a moment — then suddenly leaped up. Heedless of the wound in his leg, he jumped to the floor and seized Wu Xian by the shoulders. "Wh-what did you say? You're saying she was…"
Wu Xian's assistant rushed forward to restrain him, but Wu Xian stopped him. Looking at the agitated Chen Daipeng, he said, as coldly as before: "Yes. Fifteen years ago, after Fang Chuchu was raped, she became pregnant. The girl you killed was named Fang Wang. She was fifteen years old. We tested the deceased's DNA. She was your own daughter."
Chen Daipeng's hands slowly loosened, his legs gave way, and he collapsed to the floor. He opened his mouth but no sound came out; his face looked as if he were both crying and laughing. After a long time, he suddenly burst into loud sobs. "I had a daughter — I had a daughter! All my life, my greatest wish was to have a child of my own! I really did have a child of my own! And I burned her alive with my own hands — with my own hands — with my own hands!"
With that, Chen Daipeng raised his hand and — smack — slapped his own face, then again… His hands took turns slapping his face, harder and harder, more and more frantically.
Wu Xian didn't move. He quietly watched Chen Daipeng beat himself for half an hour, until his face was so swollen he was unrecognizable and his arms were too tired to lift. Only then did Wu Xian give his assistant a look. The assistant understood, took out a pair of handcuffs, and cuffed Chen Daipeng's hands.
