Epilogue
"Fang Wang, in the end Mom couldn't help leaving you a few words. Mom always taught you to be tolerant, kind, and loving, but Mom couldn't do it herself. Mom couldn't let go of her obsession and start life over, and that is what led to this tragedy.
"I am a failure as a mother. I couldn't fulfill a mother's duty and be there as you grew up, and I couldn't set an example and teach you kindness. That's why I named you Fang Wang — wang, 'to forget' — hoping you could forget your mother and be a good person."
When he had finished reading the letter, Fei Qiang took out a lighter and, before the headstones of Tang Sui, Fang Chuchu, and Fang Wang, set the paper alight. Watching Fang Chuchu's last words slowly turn to ash, he sighed. "Fang Wang did well. In the last moments of her life, she was still trying to save a stranger."
It was April in the north, the season of the Qingming festival. The cold had passed; the grass on the ground was putting out new shoots, and the flower growers outside the cemetery were enjoying their busiest time of the year. A steady stream of people came to the cemetery to lay flowers and sweep the graves of family and friends they had lost.
Standing before the graves of Fang Chuchu, her mother, and her daughter — three generations — Fei Qiang read Fang Chuchu's last words and recalled the appalling serial murders of a few months before. Even in the noonday sun, he couldn't help shivering. It was just like a horror novel.
The most terrifying thing in this world is not ghosts, nor murderers. It is a twisted society that turns ordinary people into murderers.
On the other side of the cemetery, a young man had come with a bunch of flowers to visit a friend who had died. Four black characters were carved on the headstone: "Our Beloved Daughter, Gu Qing." The young man laid down the flowers, opened his phone, and set it in front of the grave. A video began to play on the screen:
A middle-aged man was tied to a classroom chair with his hands behind his back, blood seeping from his belly. With a face full of terror, he said to the camera: "Gu Qing, I — I'm sorry. Back then I gave in to lust and wicked thoughts, and in the end I killed you by accident… I'm truly sorry, Gu Qing. Please forgive me."
When the video finished, the young man stood up and left. In front of the headstone he had left a bunch of flowers, and a long bamboo flute.
