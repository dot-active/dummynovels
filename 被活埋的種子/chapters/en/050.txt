Du Qiping's Prototype — Du Shaoping, Principal Culprit in the "Sports Ground Burial Case"
https://j.eastday.com/m/1641212888043793

In mid-April 2019, during a special campaign against organized crime, the Public Security Bureau of Xinhuang County, Huaihua, Hunan Province, broke up a criminal gang led by Du Shaoping and appealed widely to the public for information. In May, while checking leads on the murder of Deng Shiping referred to them by the 16th Central Inspection Team of the anti-organized-crime campaign, the Huaihua Municipal Public Security Bureau found that Du Shaoping was closely linked to the case. After an in-depth investigation, and acting on the confessions of Du Shaoping and Luo Guangzhong and their identification of the site, the police in the early hours of June 20 dug up human remains from the sports ground of Xinhuang No. 1 Middle School. DNA testing confirmed they were the remains of Deng Shiping. The case drew wide public attention and became known as the "Sports Ground Burial Case." The National Office for the Campaign Against Organized Crime listed it as a key supervised case. The Hunan Provincial Party Committee attached great importance to it, demanding that it be handled strictly and swiftly according to party discipline and the law, and that everyone involved — along with the "protective umbrellas" and "networks of connections" behind them — be investigated to the very end, with no leniency. The relevant departments of Hunan Province, Huaihua City, and Xinhuang County promptly set up a task force to investigate thoroughly the Xinhuang "Sports Ground Burial Case," the case of Du Shaoping's criminal gang, and the "protective umbrellas" and "networks of connections" behind them.

In 2001, Du Shaoping, a laid-off worker in Xinhuang County, used improper means to win, in violation of regulations, the contract for the civil works on the sports ground of Xinhuang No. 1 Middle School, and hired Luo Guangzhong and others to manage the work. During construction, Du Shaoping came to resent Deng Shiping, who was supervising the project's quality and safety on behalf of the school, and nursed a grudge. On January 22, 2003, together with Luo Guangzhong, he killed Deng Shiping and buried the body in a pit in the school's sports ground. After the crime, Huang Bingsong, then principal of Xinhuang No. 1 Middle School (and Du Shaoping's uncle), solicited help from many quarters and won over and corrupted public officials in order to cover up the murder. Yang Jun, then deputy party secretary and political commissar of the Xinhuang County Public Security Bureau (and Du Shaoping's former classmate), and others accepted these requests and interfered with, misled, and obstructed the investigation, so that the case went unsolved for many years.
