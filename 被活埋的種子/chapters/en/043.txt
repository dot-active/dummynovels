The Devoted Gardener, Part One
First pockmark hangs herself, second pockmark watches.
Third pockmark buys medicine, fourth pockmark brews it.
Fifth pockmark nails the coffin, sixth pockmark carries it.
Seventh pockmark digs the hole, eighth pockmark buries.
Ninth pockmark sits on the ground and cries.
Tenth pockmark asks him why he cries.
Ninth pockmark says:
"It wants to climb back out of the earth."
The script was at last nearing its end. At Tiecheng No. 4 Middle School's pre-exam rally for the fourth-year students, Hui Nan, facing the whole school, had spoken the rhyme's final line.
While Chen Daipeng was still reeling from those words, Hui Nan swiftly drew a dagger from her pocket and slashed his leg hard. Chen Daipeng cried out, clutched the wound, and dropped to his knees in pain.
Hui Nan stood up, put the dagger to Chen Daipeng's throat, and spoke into the microphone. "Everyone stay where you are. Don't move. If anyone dares come up onto this platform, my second cut opens his throat." No one dared move.
Hui Nan lifted the microphone and cleared her throat. "First, let me introduce myself. My name is Fang Chuchu. I am the student who disappeared from this school fifteen years ago."
A roar went up from the crowd below. Fang Chuchu, the girl who had vanished in the legend, was standing on the platform.
Hui Nan ignored it and went on:
"I am not possessed by a ghost. I am Fang Chuchu herself; Hui Nan is only a false name. Fifteen years ago I was a student here, and a terrifying experience changed my life. For fifteen years I have been looking for a chance to tell the world what was done to me. Today, at last, I can expose the ugliness and evil of this school.
"It began with an activity our language teacher organized. Our language teacher back then, Ma Dahua, said it was to help us understand how compositions were marked. Every week, one student would stay behind after school to help her grade compositions. The strange thing was that the ones kept behind were always good-looking girls.
"One day I was chosen. After school she took me to the language office and gave me bread and a sausage for supper, and a glass of orange juice. I remember that orange juice was delicious. I didn't want to finish it, so I drank less than half and poured the rest into my own water bottle and put it in my schoolbag to take home for my mother.
"The problem was the orange juice. It had been drugged. Back then, the husband of your physics teacher, Xiao Jin, made illegal drugs. The sedative in that orange juice had been supplied by Xiao Jin.
"I drank the juice and soon passed out. When I came to, I was completely naked, and a middle-aged man, equally naked, was on top of me. That man was Chen Daipeng.
"Later I found out that at the time, Ma Dahua and Xiao Jin were facing dismissal after repeated complaints about beating students and demanding bribes from them. So the two of them came up with this monstrous scheme to please Chen Daipeng and save their jobs."
Another uproar rose from the teachers and students below. Chen Daipeng, on his knees, struggled to get up and protest, but the sharp blade at his throat held him still.
When the uproar died down, Hui Nan went on:
"Chen Daipeng was shocked that I woke up, because the dose was supposed to keep me asleep until he had satisfied his animal lust, and afterward I would remember nothing. But he hadn't expected that I'd drunk less than half the juice and would wake up partway through. I screamed for help in terror. In a panic, Chen Daipeng grabbed the iron mug from the desk and smashed it against my head again and again, until I passed out a second time.
"And then came something even more terrible. My cries brought the gatekeeper on duty, Du Qiping — your Uncle Du. When he saw the scene, he must have understood what had happened. But he didn't save me, and he didn't call the police. Instead he helped by building a coffin. He said that the dead, once nailed into a coffin, can't climb out. So he showed off his coffin-making skills in front of the principal, nailed me — not yet fully dead — into the coffin, and buried me in the flowerbed in front of the teaching building.
"Heh. Now you know why our principal takes such good care of the flowerbed in front of the teaching building. He has always played the gardener himself and tended it alone. He was afraid someone would discover the secret he had buried beneath it."
