Seventh-Grade Girl Says Teacher Constantly Abuses Her; Parents Send Her in With a Voice Recorder — What It Captured Is Unbearable to Hear
In all our hearts, a teacher is a devoted gardener, nurturing the flowers of the motherland; a candle of selfless dedication, burning itself to light the way for others. Because teachers are role models, their every word and deed can have a profound effect on children.
Yet in Shangluo, Shaanxi Province, a thirteen-year-old girl named Tingting desperately wants to escape her homeroom teacher. She says that place is like hell.
Tingting (a pseudonym): "Slut, idiot, moron, shameless — he always forces me to say it. He says: Don't come back next term. If you come back, I'll crush you to death in the classroom, if you can take it."
Tingting is thirteen and in the first year of junior high at the Shangdan Gaoxin School in Shangluo, Shaanxi Province. She says she has endured these insults for nearly half a year — and, unbelievably, these vile words came from the mouth of Tingting's own homeroom teacher, a teacher surnamed Wang.
