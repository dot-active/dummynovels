The Corpse That Digs Graves
In the morning, police cars drove onto the No. 4 campus yet again. Another murder. Zhang Yao's body was found in the classroom of Class 4, Grade 4, a hole gouged into his belly. Beside him lay the murder weapon: a sharp, pointed spade.
Some people pitied Zhang Yao, some were frightened by his death, and plenty more were secretly pleased. The discipline dean who had stood on the reviewing platform every morning hurling abuse during exercises had never been popular with the students, so when the police came looking for clues, few students were willing to help. The police simply sealed the Class 4, Grade 4 classroom again, as procedure required, and left with nothing. Police helplessness seemed to have become the norm, and people had begun to accept it. But Hui Nan and Zhu Hua could not. They were the only two people on that death list still alive at the school, and they knew what was coming for them next. Zhang Yao was dead, a hole dug into his belly. Seventh pockmark digs the hole, eighth pockmark buries. If the script held, one of them was going to be buried alive.
Zhu Hua, the English teacher, and Hui Nan, the geometry teacher — the two teachers left from Class 4, Grade 4 — met once more in the mathematics office. They knew this might be the last time the two of them sat together there. The mood was so heavy that for a long time neither spoke. At last Zhu Hua broke the silence. "Do you believe in ghosts?"
"No!" Hui Nan answered firmly.
Zhu Hua: "Neither do I."
Hui Nan: "Who do you think killed these people?"
Zhu Hua: "A person."
Hui Nan: "What kind of person?"
Zhu Hua: "A teacher."
Hui Nan: "A teacher?"
Zhu Hua: "I had someone ask around at the police station about the autopsy report on the physics teacher, Xiao Jin. She was drugged before she died. Judging by the timing, whoever drugged her was someone at the dinner that night."
Hui Nan: "Then it's either you or me?"
Zhu Hua: "It could be you. But you haven't been at this school long; you can't have any deep grudge against these teachers. So it's not very likely to be you."
Hui Nan: "Then who's most likely?"
Zhu Hua: "A dead person."
Hui Nan: "The dead can kill?"
Zhu Hua: "I started to suspect it when Zhai Jia went mad. To frighten a normal person into madness, she must have seen something utterly horrifying."
Hui Nan: "Like what?"
Zhu Hua: "If you saw someone who was already dead standing in front of you, wouldn't you go mad?"
Hui Nan: "Zhai Jia certainly would."
Zhu Hua: "After Zhai Jia went mad, she told me she'd seen Wang Qin on White Stone Mountain, digging her own grave and putting up her own headstone."
Hui Nan: "A corpse that sends text messages, walks out of a police station by itself, and goes up a mountain to dig its own grave and put up its own headstone? Sounds like a haunting."
Zhu Hua: "I don't believe in ghosts."
Hui Nan: "So you think Wang Qin isn't dead?"
Zhu Hua: "The answer must be on White Stone Mountain. We can go and find it. See what Wang Qin buried in her own grave."
Hui Nan: "We? You want me to go with you?"
Zhu Hua: "We're both from somewhere else. Everyone we know in Tiecheng is a teacher. None of them is going to risk going up White Stone Mountain to save our lives."
Hui Nan: "All right. I'll go with you."
Zhu Hua: "So we go this weekend?"
Hui Nan: "No! We go right now. Zhang Yao is dead; we're next. Who knows if we'll even live till the weekend."
Hui Nan suspected Zhu Hua too, of course. So she didn't want to give her time to prepare.
Zhu Hua agreed readily. The two of them didn't even bother asking for leave. Hui Nan just said a word to the other teachers in the mathematics office, then hurried off with Zhu Hua toward White Stone Mountain.
Zhu Hua had a class that afternoon. The students waiting for their English lesson went looking for her in the English office, then in the discipline office, and finally went to Principal Chen Daipeng. Chen Daipeng learned from the mathematics office that the two teachers had gone to White Stone Mountain. By the time he hurried out of the school gate after them, they were long gone.
"Principal Chen!"
As Chen Daipeng was about to return to his office, a voice called out to him. He turned and saw Liu Banxian, who had been missing for a long time.
Chen Daipeng: "You want something with me?"
Liu Banxian: "Principal Chen seems to have run into something rather unpleasant lately."
Chen Daipeng shivered slightly. "If you've got something to say, say it quickly."
Liu Banxian's mouth split into a grin, showing a mouthful of yellow teeth. "Then I won't beat around the bush. I have a few ghost-banishing implements here that the principal might find useful." With that he took out of a cloth bag a wooden sword, some scented oil, talisman paper, and a lighter.
Chen Daipeng had just reached for the wooden sword when Liu Banxian quickly added, "A thousand yuan for the lot."
Chen Daipeng: "What? A thousand yuan for this little lot?"
Liu Banxian: "This is no time to be saving money. What's worth more than your safety?"
Chen Daipeng shook his head, took out a thousand yuan in cash, and said quietly, "Don't go talking about this to anyone."
With that, he took Liu Banxian's bag of things to the garage, started his car, and drove toward White Stone Mountain.
When Zhu Hua and Hui Nan analyzed who had been at Xiao Jin's last dinner, they had both overlooked something. Of those who attended that dinner and were still alive, besides the two of them there was Suo Xin, who had fled to another city and not been heard from since — and the principal who had invited everyone to the dinner: Chen Daipeng.
