Murder and Manslaughter
At the foot of White Stone Mountain, Fei Qiang told Hui Nan the truth about the deaths of Jia Shi and Zhai Jia.
In fact, Jia Shi had never gone abroad, nor had he poisoned himself. He had had Uncle Du build him a coffin and lain down in it only to fake his death. It was all a show staged by the deputy police chief, Jia Lian, with his son's help.
The police knew this was a tough case and that the killer was skilled, so to keep Jia Shi safe they had him fake his death, hoping to fool the killer. At the same time, Jia Shi's "death" told all the other teachers that running away was useless, which kept them at the school as bait to lure the killer into striking again. To solve the case, they had used living people as bait. Hui Nan felt like swearing, but in the end she swallowed her anger and listened as Fei Qiang went on.
After Jia Shi faked his death, the first victim was the missing Tang Sui, and for a long time after that the killer didn't strike. The police stepped up their manpower and kept round-the-clock, one-on-one watch on the potential victims on the death list, but after many days with nothing to show for it, they had to give up committing so many officers. Meanwhile, Jia Shi had been stuck at home for a month after faking his death and was bored out of his mind. He couldn't sit still, and it so happened that a friend of his who worked as a courier fell ill and asked Jia Shi to cover a shift for him. One of the deliveries, by sheer coincidence, ended up in Zhai Jia's hands.
The card delivered to Zhai Jia had actually been sent to her boyfriend, A Cheng, by his secret lover. It was the kind of little romantic game young women like to play, but the paranoid Zhai Jia read it as a summons from the King of Hell. "The other side of the bridge" was simply the place where the two of them met in secret, but Zhai Jia had thought of the Bridge of Helplessness.
When Jia Shi realized he'd been recognized and couldn't shake off Zhai Jia, who was following him, he decided to scare her away. So he walked all the way to White Stone Mountain and lay down in a coffin, thinking it would frighten her off. He never imagined that Zhai Jia, terrified beyond reason, would do something completely out of character: she picked up a large rock and attacked him. And so Jia Shi really did die in that coffin — Zhai Jia beat him to death with the rock, blow after blow. At the same time, the horrifying, bloody scene unhinged her, and she became a madwoman.
Hearing this, Hui Nan couldn't stop sighing with regret. Two innocent young people, because of paranoia and superstition, had brought about such a terrible tragedy. But what happened next turned truly evil.
After Jia Shi's body was found, the higher-ups in the police bureau gave orders to find the killer as quickly as possible, whatever the cost.
After Uncle Du died, when the police interrogated Zhai Jia, they finally uncovered the truth of Jia Shi's death. But the police didn't close the case. Zhai Jia was obviously mentally ill; if she were prosecuted, it would be hard to convict someone insane in court, and she would most likely end up in a psychiatric hospital. The police were never going to let the person who had killed the deputy chief's son off so lightly. So they released Zhai Jia — and gave her a dagger. Of course, the dagger was never going to kill anyone. It was only meant to give the police an excuse to shoot her. And sure enough, not long after leaving the police station, Zhai Jia died under Chief Wu Xian's gun.
Jia Shi's death, seemingly eerie and mysterious, had in fact been manslaughter. And Zhai Jia's death, seemingly an accident, had been a carefully planned murder.
The more Fei Qiang talked, the more pained he looked; by the end he was almost speaking through gritted teeth. A young man's ideals had been smashed to pieces by reality.
Hui Nan looked at him with sympathy, and for the first time there was tenderness and admiration in her eyes. Fei Qiang had only chosen to resign and walk away, but in that environment, holding on to the conscience a human being ought to have was no small thing.
After hearing the truth from Fei Qiang, Hui Nan was no longer so worried about Zhu Hua, still up on the mountain. Zhai Jia had gone mad because of Jia Shi; so there was nothing frightening on White Stone Mountain, and naturally no danger there either.
But Hui Nan was wrong. At that moment Zhu Hua was at the summit, going through something even more horrifying than what Zhai Jia had suffered.
