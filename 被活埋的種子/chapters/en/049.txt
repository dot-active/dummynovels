Xiao Jin's Prototype — Teacher Xiao of Tianjin
https://m.sohu.com/a/453240339_568437/?pvid=000115_3w_a

On February 25, an internet user revealed that a teacher at a middle school in Tianjin had discriminated against a student while lecturing her: "How much does your mother earn? Don't blame me for looking down on you — one student's mother earns more in a year than your mother earns in fifty."
In the recording, the female teacher says that the students sent to her class in the past all had parents who were officials, or came from especially well-off families.
"How much does your mother earn a month? Don't blame me for looking down on you. Let me tell you, XXX's mother earns more in one year than your mother earns in fifty."
Once the recording was made public, it immediately set off heated discussion online.
On February 26, the WeChat public account "Jinnan Education News" issued a statement.
"On February 25, 2021, it was reported online that a teacher in Tianjin's Jinnan District, Xiao X, had made inappropriate remarks while disciplining students. The district education bureau immediately set up an investigation team to verify the reports. The teacher has now been suspended. The education bureau will deal with the matter strictly, in accordance with laws and regulations, on the basis of the investigation's findings, and will promptly make the results public."
